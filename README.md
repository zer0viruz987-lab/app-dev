### To start with my three favourite Movies.

**The Green Mile** 
One of the *best* movies that I've watched as a young adult. It showed me how much
cruelty a man can take, that no matter how special and how innocent you may be, there
will always be someone that will not take you for who you are. And that the only people
that will know you truly is the people who you interact with, people that understood and 
knew that who you truly are.

The best scene in my opinion is the fact that John Coffey, the main protagonist is tired
of how cruel humans can be. That it wouldn't matter if he was set free by the police. 
Because he knew that there will always be a time that man will show cruelty in the face 
of innocence.

**The Nightmare on Elm Street**
The *Nightmare on Elm Street* is a movie where people face their deepest fears, played 
out by a sadistic demon-like entity called Freddy Krueger. This movie shows the depths
of how man can fear, and at the same time be able to face those fears.

**Men in Black**
Just an overall fun and hilarious movie. It's a nice action driven and thrilling movie
that revolves around two agents and their journey to fend off their local aliens.
